What every package covers

You see what is included before you pay, and anything optional is listed separately.

  • FlightsReturn international flights, with domestic legs and airport transfers where the route needs them.
  • StayHotels or resorts with breakfast, chosen by location first and then by budget.
  • Visa and documentsChecklist, form filling and appointment support. Government visa fees are shown separately. See visa rules by country.
  • SightseeingPrivate or shared transfers and guided tours as listed in each day's plan.
  • Travel insuranceOptional cover for medical costs, cancellations and baggage. Required for the Schengen visa.
  • Support during travelA contact number that works while you are abroad, not only at office hours back home.
